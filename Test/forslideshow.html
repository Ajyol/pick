Poetry of Brenda Marie Osbey

Brenda Marie Osbey is a well known and respected poet from Louisiana. She was appointed Poet Laureate of the state in 2005. While Osbey tried not to dedicate many poems focusing on the disaster of Hurrican Katrina, she continues to write about her memories, experiences, and grievances about her home town of New Orleans, LA. She is also an esteemed writer of African history in the U.S. She is currently a professor of Africana Studies at Brown University. This site is dedicated to her poetry as a literary authority on southern culture and literature.
